# Extract Brand English

A skill designed to extract high-frequency, idiomatic, and professional English expressions and collocations from source text.

## When to Use

- Explicit Trigger: When the user mentions `extract-brand-english`.
- Implicit Trigger: When the user provides an English text (articles, guidelines, venue policies, brand manuals) and asks to extract practical, high-frequency, and professional English expressions.

## Instructions

1. **Direct Output**:
   - Start directly with the extracted content in sentence one.
   - Omit generic opening greetings, filler introductions, and meta-commentary (e.g., do not say "Here are the expressions extracted for you").

2. **Categorized Structure**:
   - Organize the extracted expressions into 3 to 5 distinct thematic categories based on the source text domain (e.g., *Facilities & Spaces*, *Service Policies & Operations*, *Accessibility & Inclusion*, *Layout & Brand Assets*).
   - Use Markdown H3 headings (`###`) or standalone bold headings for each category.

3. **Format per Entry**:
   - Highlight the authentic English expression or phrase pattern in **bold**.
   - Provide an accurate, concise Chinese translation in parentheses directly beside or below the expression.
   - Include a brief note on practical usage context or common collocations where applicable.

4. **High-Frequency Glossary**:
   - Conclude with a dedicated section titled `### High-Frequency Phrases & Collocations` (高频通用搭配备用).
   - List versatile fixed prepositions, business idiomatic phrases, or operational conditions from the text (e.g., *Subject to availability*, *Available upon request*, *Free of charge*).
